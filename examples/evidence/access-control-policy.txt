Access Control Policy
Revision 4.2 — effective 2026-06-01 — owner: Head of Security

1. Purpose
The organisation restricts logical access to protected information assets to
authorised users, and restricts the assets those users may reach to what their
role requires.

2. Registration and authorisation
Authorised users are registered in the identity provider before any access is
granted. Access requests are approved by the asset owner, recorded in the
service desk, and issued on a least-privilege basis. Role definitions and their
entitlements are documented and reviewed quarterly.

3. Authentication
Multi-factor authentication is required for all remote access, for all
administrative accounts, and for any access to systems holding regulated data.
Shared credentials are prohibited. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.

4. Removal
Access is removed within one business day of a role change or departure.
Removal is verified against the leavers report, not assumed from the ticket.

5. Physical access
Access to facilities holding infrastructure is badge-controlled and logged.
Visitors are escorted throughout.
