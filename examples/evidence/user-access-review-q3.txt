User Access Review — Q3 2026
Completed 2026-07-14 — reviewer: Compliance Manager — approver: Head of Security

Scope: every application holding client or regulated data, plus the identity
provider itself and all infrastructure administration consoles.

Method: entitlement export per application, reconciled against the authorised
user register and the current org chart. Each line was reviewed by the asset
owner, who recorded a sign-off decision of retain, reduce or remove.

Findings and disposition:
- 4 accounts belonging to leavers were still enabled in a reporting tool.
  Removed the same day; the leavers process was amended to include that tool.
- 11 users held entitlements broader than their role required. Reduced to
  least privilege.
- Multi-factor authentication enforcement was verified for every administrative
  account in scope. No exceptions found.
- Shared credentials: none found.

Reviewer sign-off recorded for every application in scope. Evidence retained:
entitlement exports, sign-off records, and the change tickets raised.
