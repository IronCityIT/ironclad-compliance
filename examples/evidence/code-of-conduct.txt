Code of Conduct and Ethics Policy
Revision 2.1 — effective 2026-01-05 — owner: Managing Director

Commitment: the organisation commits to integrity and ethical values in all
dealings with clients, staff and suppliers. The board sets the tone at the top
and is accountable for the control environment.

Standards of conduct: honesty in client reporting, confidentiality of client
information, refusal of conflicts of interest, and prompt disclosure where one
arises.

Acknowledgement: every member of staff acknowledges this code on joining and
annually thereafter. Acknowledgement records are retained. The 2026 cycle
completed on 2026-01-31 with full coverage.

Reporting: a confidential reporting channel is available to staff and to
clients. Reports are triaged by the Managing Director, or by the board where
the Managing Director is implicated. Retaliation is a disciplinary matter.

Training: annual security awareness and ethics training is mandatory. The 2026
cycle completed on 2026-03-10; completion is tracked to individual level.
