Incident Response Plan
Revision 3.0 — effective 2026-02-15 — owner: Head of Security

Phases: detection, triage, containment, eradication, recovery, and a
post-incident review held within ten working days of closure.

Detection sources: security monitoring alerts, log review, client report, and
staff report. Every source routes to the same intake queue so nothing depends
on which door an incident arrives through.

Escalation: a documented severity matrix maps impact and scope onto a severity,
and severity onto who is woken. The on-call rota is published a month ahead and
tested by the monthly out-of-hours call exercise.

Communication: a notification plan covers affected clients, the regulator where
notification is required, and staff. Client notification templates are held
with the plan and are reviewed by counsel annually.

Testing: an annual tabletop exercise was completed 2026-04-22, covering a
ransomware scenario and a third-party breach scenario. Findings were tracked to
closure; two remain open with target dates.

Recovery: restoration is from backup, and a restore is tested quarterly rather
than assumed from a successful backup job.
